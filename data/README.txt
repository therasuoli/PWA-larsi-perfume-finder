Perfume catalogue

- manifest.json lists every chunk (perfumes-NN.json); the app loads only what is listed there.
- Chunks are GENERATED by `node scripts/build-data.mjs` (Scento dataset + data/extra/*.json). Do not edit them by hand.
- Put your own records in data/extra/*.json (JSON array) - they survive the weekly refresh.
- data/archive/ holds untouched raw originals and is never loaded by the app.
- Validate with `node scripts/validate-data.mjs` (fails on orphan chunks, count mismatch, duplicate ids).
