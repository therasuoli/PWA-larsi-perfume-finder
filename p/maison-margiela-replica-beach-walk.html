Replica Beach Walk

A sunny beach scent: bergamot, pink pepper and lemon, then ylang ylang, heliotrope and coconut milk, over cedarwood, white musks and benzoin. Fresh, creamy and sun-warmed. بهترین فصل استفاده: بهار، تابستان، پاییز.
