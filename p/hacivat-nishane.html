A citrus-chypre scent: bergamot, pineapple and grapefruit give a juicy opening, jasmine, patchouli and cedarwood form the heart, and clearwood, oakmoss and dry timberwood anchor it. بهترین فصل استفاده: بهار، تابستان، پاییز.
