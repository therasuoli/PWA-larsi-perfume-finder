Black Aoud

A dark oud perfume lifted by lemon, mandarin and saffron, with rose and patchouli at the centre and leather, guaiac wood and sandalwood underneath. Smoky, spicy and bold. بهترین فصل استفاده: بهار، تابستان، پاییز.
