Red Aoud

Citrus and lavender open onto rose, violet and patchouli. The base turns sweet and deep, with vanilla, toffee, tonka, oud, musk and amber, for a rich, warm finish. بهترین فصل استفاده: بهار، تابستان، پاییز.
