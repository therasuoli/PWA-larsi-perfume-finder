Ani

Bergamot, blue ginger and pink pepper open into blackcurrant, Turkish rose and cardamom. A vanilla-led base of benzoin, ambergris, patchouli and sandalwood makes it warm, spicy and creamy. بهترین فصل استفاده: پاییز، زمستان، بهار.
