Khamrah

A dessert-like spiced gourmand: cinnamon, nutmeg and bergamot lead into dates, praline and tuberose, then a long vanilla, tonka, myrrh and benzoin base. بهترین فصل استفاده: پاییز، زمستان، بهار.
